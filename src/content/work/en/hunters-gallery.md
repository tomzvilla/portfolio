## Context

Hunter's Gallery is a gun shop in Córdoba that sold through a personal WhatsApp, without recording anything.

## What I built

The whole system, on my own and in stages:

- **Public catalog:** customers build their order and send it to the shop's WhatsApp.
- **Internal management:** orders and invoicing with ARCA (Argentina's tax agency), from WhatsApp to the invoice.
- **WhatsApp assistant:** drafts replies to inquiries and a person approves them before they're sent.

## Built with

Next.js, Node and MongoDB, with language models for the assistant.

## Outcome

The shop went from a personal chat to a system with a catalog, records and invoicing. I've been in charge of development since 2024. Screenshots are from the public site; the internal system isn't shown.
