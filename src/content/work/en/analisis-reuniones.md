## Context

An Italian startup was building a meeting analysis platform designed for the Italian language. It joined meetings on Google Meet, Microsoft Teams and Zoom, recorded, transcribed and summarized them, picked out action items and answered questions about what was said.

## What I built

I worked on the full stack development of the product from December 2024 to October 2025.

## Built with

TypeScript, React and Node.

## Outcome

An assistant that, beyond summaries, let you generate emails, documents and recaps from one or several meetings.
