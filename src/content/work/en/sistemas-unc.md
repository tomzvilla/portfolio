## Context

Since 2023 I've worked at the IT Office (Prosecretaría de Informática, PSI) of the National University of Córdoba, the team that builds and runs the university's systems. More than 100,000 students use them every day.

## What I built

- **Authentication:** I run the university's single sign-on (SSO). I added two-factor authentication (MFA), rate limiting and upgrades to secure versions.
- **User management and self-service:** I build and maintain the student management and self-service systems.
- **Deployments:** I set up CI/CD pipelines so shipping a release doesn't depend on manual steps.
- **Legacy code:** I refactored and optimized old systems so they respond faster.
- **AI assistants:** a smaller part of my work, covered in [Assistants for the UNC](/en/work/asistentes-unc/).

## Built with

Vue (Vuex, Vue Router), Node, Express and MongoDB; SAML for single sign-on.

## Outcome

More than three years keeping the systems the whole student community relies on running. The details are in my [CV](/cv/Villarreal-Tomas-CV-EN.pdf).
