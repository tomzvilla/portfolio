## Context

Cameras detect possible traffic violations, and every detection has to be validated by a person before it moves forward.

## What I built

In a team of three, I handled the frontend of the review portal: device management, coverage maps and validation flows based on each user's role.

## Built with

React on the frontend, on top of a NestJS API.

## Outcome

A portal where each person sees and validates what their role calls for. Confidential project: no names, no screenshots.
